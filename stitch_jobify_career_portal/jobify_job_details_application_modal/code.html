terminal Role Overview & Mission

As a Frontend Developer Intern at Vercel Labs, you will sit at the intersection of production-grade design systems, runtime compiler surfaces, and bleeding-edge user interfaces. You will join our Core DX team for 12 intense weeks to architect, benchmark, and deploy mission-critical surfaces relied on by over 2.4 million cloud engineers worldwide.

We treat our interns exactly as junior-to-mid career full-time software engineers. You will push code directly to production, review architecture RFCs, lead bi-weekly engineering brownbags, and build tangible software artifacts that live well past your cohort term.
